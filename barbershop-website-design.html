Frequently asked questions

Do you work with Booksy, TheCut and Square?+

Yes. The website links straight to the booking profile of the shop or of each individual barber. We do not replace the platform you already work with.

Can I update barbers, photos and hours myself?+

Yes. On websites with an owner panel you make changes from a computer or a phone and they go live in a minute or two. The design stays locked, so nothing can break.

Do you work with shops in the United States?+

Yes. Two of our barbershop projects are in the USA — 210 Barber Studio in San Antonio and Exclusive Barbershop. We work remotely from Sofia, in English.

How much does it cost and how long does it take?+

It depends on the number of barbers, the sections and whether you want an owner panel. You receive a written quote with price and timeline before we start.
