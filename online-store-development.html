Frequently asked questions

Which payment methods do you support?+

We integrate Stripe for card payments as standard. Other methods are agreed in the quote.

How many products can the store have?+

There is no technical limit. The number of products and integrations affect the scope and are set out in the quote.

Can you automate invoices and deliveries?+

Yes. For New Shade we built a system that issues documents automatically and tracks orders and deliveries in one place.
