CASE STUDY · 210 BARBER STUDIO

A website for a
17-barber studio.

210 Barber Studio is a 17-barber shop in San Antonio. The website helps a visitor pick their barber and sends them straight to booking — or invites them to walk in.

Visit the live website↗Request a quote
Industry
Barbershop
Location
San Antonio, Texas, USA
Scope
Website · Booking · Owner panel

The brief

Each of the 17 barbers has their own clients, prices and booking page. The website had to show the whole team in one place, take a visitor to the right barber in one tap, and present the competition trophies that set the shop apart. The owner also needed to add barbers and photos without waiting for a developer.

What we built

Related service: Barbershop website design →

  1. 01Choose your barberA card for every barber with a starting price and a direct booking link — Booksy, Square or theCut. Sortable by price.
  2. 02Haircut galleryThe “Fresh Out the Chair” section with full-screen view and a caption for every cut.
  3. 03Awards and competitionsA separate gallery for the team’s trophies and competition appearances.
  4. 04One-tap contactOpening hours, tap-to-call and tap-for-directions, plus links to Instagram, TikTok and Facebook.
  5. 05Owner panelThe shop updates barbers, photos, opening hours and texts on its own — from a computer or a phone. The design stays locked, and changes go live in a minute or two.
  6. 06Editing guideA short illustrated guide so the team can use the panel without training.
