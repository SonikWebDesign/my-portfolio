Frequently asked questions

Do you guarantee first place on Google?+

No, and nobody can honestly guarantee it. We guarantee a clear scope and a report of the work done — we work on the factors that determine rankings.

What is the difference between SEO and AI search visibility?+

Classic SEO helps a page rank on Google. AI search tools summarise answers and cite sources; for them clear facts, structured data and crawler access matter most.

Do you work on websites you did not build?+

Yes. We start with an audit and propose a specific list of fixes.

When do results show?+

Technical fixes are picked up within weeks, while rankings move over a few months. It also depends on the competition in your niche.
